Patrick Wales-Dinan

Head Coach

Patrick Wales-Dinan is the head cross country and track and field coach at Monte Vista High School in Danville, California, and the head coach of the Bay Area Track Club. He has coached distance runners for two decades, from high school through NCAA Division I. His athletes have won national championships, earned All-American honors and qualified for the United States Olympic Trials.

He began coaching in 2006 at Traip Academy, a high school in Kittery, Maine.

College coaching

In 2007 he joined the University of Southern Maine as an assistant coach for cross country and track and field.

From 2009 to 2011 he was an assistant coach at Williams College, where he coached the women's distance group to five All-American awards and two national championships in two seasons. He coached Jennifer Gossels to the NCAA Division III cross country runner-up finish in 2010, and to the 2011 NCAA Division III titles in the 5,000 and 10,000 meters in outdoor track.

In 2011 and 2012 he was an assistant coach for women's cross country and track and field at Duke University.

In 2012 he was hired as head cross country and distance coach at Long Beach State University. Over two seasons he coached Chris Low to first team All-American honors in the 800 meters in 1:47, and Gabe Hilbert to a USATF championships qualifying mark, also in 1:47. He coached Chris Enriquez to individual NCAA championship berths in cross country and the outdoor 10,000 meters, making Enriquez the first Long Beach State distance runner to qualify individually for the NCAA Cross Country Championships.

In 2014 he joined Harvard University as associate head coach, leading women's cross country and coaching distance runners in men's and women's track and field. Over three seasons, through 2017, he led the Harvard women to the 2016 Ivy League Cross Country Championship and was named Ivy League Women's Cross Country Coach of the Year. The team advanced to the NCAA Championships that fall, where Courtney Smith earned All-American honors. On the track he coached two Olympic Trials qualifiers: Smith ran 32:08 for 10,000 meters, the second-ranked time in the NCAA that season, and Paige Kouba ran 9:50 in the steeplechase.

Monte Vista High School

Patrick was hired at Monte Vista High School in 2018. Under him, Monte Vista cross country has become one of the most consistent programs in Northern California.

The girls won NCS Division 2 championships in 2022, 2024 and 2025, including back-to-back section titles, and finished runner-up in 2019, 2021 and 2023. They won EBAL league titles in 2024 and 2026. From 2019 to 2025, Monte Vista girls earned 15 individual top-10 finishes at the NCS Division 2 Championships, more than any other school in the division. Alexandra Powell (2024) and Kaylie O'Connell (2025) won NCS individual titles, giving Monte Vista six different NCS individual champions, the most of any school in the section. In 2025, three of the section's top six finishers were Monte Vista sophomores: O'Connell first, Nadya Makarova fifth and Ava Padilla sixth.

The girls have qualified for the CIF State Championships as a team in every season of his tenure, part of a streak of 12 straight State appearances dating to 2013. The 2024 team placed seventh in Division II. Kelli Wilson, Allie Turns and Alexa Leahey each qualified for State all four years. Wilson placed 16th in Division II in 2019, and Powell placed 15th in 2023. At the 2026 Clovis Invitational, O'Connell ran 17:33.5 and Makarova 17:40.2, the second and third fastest times by Monte Vista girls at Woodward Park, and the team's 1:32:12 is the fastest by any Mustang girls team on the State course.

The boys finished third at NCS in 2019 and runner-up in 2021, earned eight Division 2 top-10 finishes from 2019 to 2025, and qualified for State as a team in 2019, 2021 and 2022, placing 10th in Division II in 2021. Ethan Miller placed sixth in Division II at State in 2022 in 15:15.5, the fastest State time in school history. Mateo Cafaro placed second at NCS in 2025 and 25th at State in 15:20.8, the second fastest State time by a Monte Vista boy. Rohan Kotamraju placed fourth at NCS in 2025 and ran 15:30.5 at the 2026 Clovis Invitational.

In all, his teams have sent 42 different athletes to the CIF State Championships for a total of 72 State races.

Winter training

Monday, December 14 through Saturday, February 6 — eight weeks, ending the weekend before track and field practice begins.

Training runs Monday through Saturday.

  • School days: 15 minutes after school lets out, at Oak Hill Park, Danville
  • School holidays: Oak Hill Park, time announced in advance
  • Saturdays: Iron Horse Trailhead in Alamo, or Independence Park in Livermore — location announced each week

We will not meet on December 24, 25 or 26. Training continues on your own those days.

Cost: $500

Patrick Wales-Dinan

Head Coach

Patrick Wales-Dinan is the head coach of the Bay Area Track Club and the head cross country and track and field coach at Monte Vista High School in Danville, California. He has coached distance runners for two decades, from high school through NCAA Division I, and his athletes have won national championships, earned All-American honors and qualified for the United States Olympic Trials.
